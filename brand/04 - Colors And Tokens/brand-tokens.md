## Typography

- UI and editorial text: **Inter**, weights Regular / Medium / SemiBold / Bold.
- Display wordmark: the supplied wordmark assets define the brand appearance. The outlined SVGs contain their own glyph paths, while the PNGs preserve the raster source's metallic detail. Inter is the supporting UI and documentation typeface.
- No fonts or font binaries are packaged. Obtain Inter from its official distributor under its own license.
- Suggested UI: headline 28–36 px; H2 20–24 px; body 14–16 px; captions 12–13 px.

## Contrast and accessible status

Use color and clear written status labels together. Do not communicate secure/authenticated solely through blue, green, or a checked icon. Maintain sufficient text contrast in actual components and across supported screens.
